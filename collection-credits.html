File:Semuliki National Park Vegetation.jpg

Timothy Akolamazima · CC BY-SA 4.0

Semuliki National Park is home to one of East Africa's oldest tropical lowland forests, part of the Congo Basin ecosystem. Its diverse vegetation includes dense evergreen forests, swamp forests, riverine woodlands, aquatic sedge meadows, and grasslands that support a rich variety of birds, primates, forest elephants, and other wildlife. In the background, the northern foothills of the Rwenzori Mountains rise above the landscape, helping to capture moisture that sustains the park's lush vegetation and remarkable biodiversity, making Semuliki one of Uganda's most ecologically significant protected areas.
