Wetland Pool at Semuliki

Timothy Akolamazima · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Original source

Uganda — source-described. Resized proportionally for web where needed; photographs cropped responsively in CSS; illustration panels retain their full composition.

Semuliki National Park is home to one of East Africa's oldest tropical lowland forests, part of the Congo Basin ecosystem. Its diverse vegetation includes dense evergreen forests, swamp forests, riverine woodlands, aquatic sedge meadows, and grasslands that support a rich variety of birds, primates, forest elephants, and other wildlife. In the background, the northern foothills of the Rwenzori Mountains rise above the landscape, helping to capture moisture that sustains the park's lush vegetation and remarkable biodiversity, making Semuliki one of Uganda's most ecologically significant protected areas. Identification and location follow the source; presence in Uganda is not inferred from an illustration.

Semuliki forest Boardwalk

Pearl Aketch · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Original source

Uganda — source-described. Resized proportionally for web where needed; photographs cropped responsively in CSS; illustration panels retain their full composition.

This is the wooden boardwalk that visitors reach after walking through the forest from the female hot spring at Sempaya, on the way to the male hot spring, inside Semuliki National Park, Bundibugyo District. Semuliki is part of the Congo Basin rainforest system and is the only national park in Uganda made up entirely of lowland tropical forest, home to primates like red-tailed monkeys and black-and-white colobus, along with a wide range of bird species. The boardwalk was built to let visitors cross the swampy ground between the two hot springs without disturbing the wetland underneath. Identification and location follow the source; presence in Uganda is not inferred from an illustration.
