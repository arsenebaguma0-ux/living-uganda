ABOUT THE INITIATIVE

For everyone
who wonders.

An independent resource about Uganda’s living world: organisms, peoples, cultures and their connections.

A familiar place to learn about nature

The aim is a resource people remember and consult when curious about a plant, animal, fungus or another organism. Accessible names, clear descriptions and useful images should make knowledge easier to explore.

This initiative stands independently of the Murchison Falls landscape research and Uganda biodiversity-priority assessment. “Living Uganda” is the provisional name. The focus is life and biological knowledge, including people and their cultural relationships with other organisms; geographical features and archaeological sites are outside the main scope.

How information will be handled

01

Evidence for Uganda

Document occurrence using Uganda-specific sources. Distinguish native or wild, introduced or naturalised, cultivated or captive, and uncertain occurrence.

02

Sources people can follow

Reference biological claims, date conservation assessments, separate global and national status, and show review dates.

03

Attributed local knowledge

Record names with their language, region and source. Credit cultural accounts and distinguish them from biological evidence. Never invent names or stories.

04

Respect for photographs

Use photographs with documented permission or reusable licences, retaining required credits and links.

A place to follow your questions.

Explore species, conservation, living relationships, peoples and cultures, and the evidence behind what we know. This independent resource grows through careful research and clear attribution.
